project builds with nothing but Visual Studio and `build.bat`. The release
package additionally ships a third-party ASI loader.

## Zydis

<https://github.com/zyantific/zydis> - MIT. Pulled in by safetyhook as its
disassembler. The full MIT text is kept in the header of `Zydis.h` and `Zydis.c`
as generated by the amalgamation.

## Ultimate ASI Loader

<https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader> - MIT License.

The release package ships `winmm.dll` from this project. It is what loads
`plugins/BrethrenPatch.asi`; the patch itself does not depend on it and will
load through any ASI loader.
